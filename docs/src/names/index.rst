The names
=========

senfd reads the **S**\ pecification documents, **E**\ xtracts and
**N**\ ormalises their **F**\ igures, and makes a **D**\ ata model.
``sate`` is **S**\ enfd **A**\ sk **T**\ he **E**\ xpert. *Senf* is
the colloquial Danish word for mustard, taken from German; the standard
Danish word is *sennep*. *Senf* is the word a customer uses at a
*pølsevogn*, a Danish hot dog stand, to ask for a sausage with mustard:
"pølse med senf". The logo is a mustard flower with four petals in mustard
yellow. In the large logo, the petals show documents, a table, two
blocks merging into one, and a gear, and the centre shows a hierarchy, the
data model. A sate is a dish of marinated meat.
